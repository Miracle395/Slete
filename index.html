Answers.

Slete is a precision prediction market built on DeepBook Predict on Sui. Instead of binary up/down bets, you name the exact closing price of an asset. The predictor closest to the real oracle price wins the entire prize pool, there are no splits, no house edge on outcomes.

Each pool has a target asset (currently BTC/USDC), an entry fee in DUSDC and a snapshot time. You submit your predicted closing price and stake DUSDC to enter. When the snapshot time hits, the DeepBook oracle records the actual price onchain. The predictor whose number is closest wins everything in the pool.

DUSDC is the testnet stablecoin used on DeepBook Predict it's a 1:1 USD-pegged token on Sui Testnet. You can receive DUSDC directly from the Slete team or DeepBook team. Since this is testnet, there's no real money at stake, it's perfect for learning the mechanics before mainnet.

Slete is currently live on Sui Testnet, running on DeepBook Predict's testnet deployment. All predictions, entries and payouts use testnet DUSDC. Mainnet launch will follow once the protocol is fully audited and battle tested.

Settlement is fully onchain and trustless. At snapshot time, a DeepBook oracle price feed records the asset price directly into the DeepBook Predict smart contract. The contract then calculates which prediction is numerically closest and releases the pool to that address automatically.

Each wallet can submit more than one prediction per pool. This keeps the competition skill based, you commit to a price, you back it with stake and you live with the result. Multiple pools run simultaneously with different snapshot times, so you can be active across several markets at once.
